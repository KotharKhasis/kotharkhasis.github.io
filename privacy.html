Qart: New City
Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

In short: Qart does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your game saves stay on your device.

Who we are

Qart: New City ("the game") is made by KotharKhasis Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play the game on Android.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for any Android permissions and does not connect to the internet. We do not receive any information about you or how you play.

Your game saves

Your saved games are stored only on your device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If your device's own backup service is turned on, Android may include them in your device backup; that backup is handled by your device's provider under its own terms, not by us.

Purchases

If the game offers an in-app purchase, it is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells us only that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock what you bought.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example anonymous crash and performance reports for developers. We do not control that information.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding an online feature, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
